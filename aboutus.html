About Us:

CHARUSAT (Charotar University of Science and Technology) is a premier NAAC A+ accredited and UGC-approved private university located in Changa, Anand district, Gujarat, India. Established in 2009 under the Gujarat Private University Act, it traces its roots back to 2000 with the launch of its first engineering institute. Managed by the non-profit trust Shri Charotar Moti Sattavis Patidar Kelavani Mandal, the university spans a sprawling 125-acre green campus.

Our Top Faculties

CHARUSAT organizes its academic structure into 6 major faculties (encompassing 9 distinct institutes). In Indian university terminology, "faculties" refer to the core academic divisions/departments rather than individual teaching staff members
